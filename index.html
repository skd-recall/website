About The Project

BUILT BY
STUDENTS.

Kinai began as a Final Year Project at CECOS University Peshawar — born from a simple observation: access to quality fitness training and physiotherapy in Pakistan is expensive, geographically limited, and inconsistent.

Our team of three set out to bridge that gap using artificial intelligence. The result is FitBot — a smart mobile fitness companion — and the concept for Kinai Medical, an AI physiotherapy module that keeps patients recovering safely at home.

Both applications are powered by computer vision, generative AI, and machine learning — running entirely on a standard smartphone camera with no additional hardware required.
